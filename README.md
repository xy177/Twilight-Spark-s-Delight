# Twilight Spark's Delight

![Twilight Spark's Delight](arts/LargeLogo_github.png)

## Overview

**Twilight Spark's Delight** is an original Farmer's Delight addon built around the world of The Twilight Forest.

The forest's beasts, beetles, boss trophies, and mysterious experiments can all find their way into new recipes. Hunt, cut, pickle, and cook your ingredients, then serve dishes large enough to share with the whole table. You can also study Experiment 250, work with the Glory Crucible, collect food in Glass Jars, or build a kitchen fit for a giant.

The mod also includes new potion effects, placeable feasts, advancements, JEI integration, and several optional mod compatibilities.

## New Feasts

**Twilight Cheese Fondue** - Light the pot, bring its dedicated Companion, and gather around a bubbling serving of Quest Ram Cheese.

**Salt-Roasted Helmet Crab** - A whole armored crab served shell and all, with plenty of tender meat and a hefty claw waiting inside.

**Twilight Borscht** - A rich, deeply colored forest stew made to sit proudly at the center of the table on a cold evening.

**Twilight Boar Knuckle** - A huge roasted knuckle stretching across its platter, with every serving cut thick and satisfying.

**Naga Mixed Rice** - A true centerpiece that fills a 3x3 space with Naga, mushrooms, forest produce, and layer after layer of ingredients.

**Abyss Pie** - Once the eye in the middle starts staring back, the tentacles around it somehow become a little easier to eat.

**Pickled Bracken Jar** - A crisp jar of forest greens made for sharing slowly, with a reusable Glass Jar left behind when the last serving is gone.

## New Potion Effects

**Charge** - Improves movement and step height, making a fast approach feel properly forceful.

**Symbiosis** - Reduces damage to players at the cost of extra hunger. On other creatures, it also provides gradual healing.

**Shrink** - Reduces the player's model, collision size, and camera scale, with a chance to evade incoming attacks.

**Enlarge** - Increases the player's model, collision size, and camera scale, adjusting movement to match the new size.

**Abyss Call** - Causes attacks to spread Symbiosis to hostile creatures.

**Sorrow** - Softens incoming damage and may transform into a higher level of Grief under special circumstances.

**Grief** - Turns accumulated sorrow into strength, increasing the damage dealt by the player.

## New Cookware

**Glory Crucible** - A large crucible made for both cooking and brewing. Fill it with Fiery Blood or Fiery Tears to process Furnace and Campfire recipes repeatedly without consuming the heated fluid. Its capacity can also be adjusted in the configuration file.

The Glory Crucible can brew an entire batch of potions at once. Pour in several matching regular, splash, or lingering potions, then add the appropriate brewing ingredient. As long as there is a heat source below, the liquid will gradually change color until the whole batch has been transformed. The finished potions can then be collected one bottle at a time. When Fruits Delight Legacy is installed, the Glory Crucible also supports its cauldron recipes.

**Giant Stove** - A 2x2x2 stove capable of cooking up to six Campfire-compatible ingredients at the same time. It is large enough to handle a busy kitchen without taking over the whole building.

**Giant Cooking Pot** - A matching 2x2x2 Cooking Pot that prepares multiple batches during a single cooking cycle. It processes up to eight batches by default, and the limit can be adjusted in the configuration file.

**Giant's Stove** - A truly oversized 4x4x4 stove built for giant-sized appetites. It can cook up to sixteen ingredients at once, provided you have enough food to fill it.

**Giant's Cooking Pot** - A 4x4x4 Cooking Pot capable of preparing up to sixteen batches in one cooking cycle.

## Advancements

Twilight Spark's Delight currently has its own advancement page containing **27 advancements**, covering forest ingredients, dangerous hunts, ambitious cooking, and shared feasts.

The mod also adds **11 hidden advancements**. Their triggers are for you, the person seated at this table, to discover...

## Extended Hunger and Saturation

The extended food system gradually raises the player's Hunger and Saturation caps from the vanilla limit of 20 to a maximum of 40. Once more capacity has been unlocked, ordinary food can fill the additional points; no special food is required.

The system is enabled by default and follows ten selected Twilight Forest progression advancements. Each completed advancement adds 2 points to both caps. The configuration file can disable progressive unlocking, grant the full capacity immediately, or turn the entire extended system off.

When AppleSkin is installed, the additional range also supports its Saturation overlay and food-value preview.
